      The Artist

      Every frame, touched with gold.

      I'm Ted Leonard — a visual storyteller based in Nairobi, Kenya, working worldwide. Since founding the studio in June 2025, I have pursued the rare intersections where light, raw emotion, and precise composition align into something timeless.

      My practice spans editorial portraiture, landscape, architecture and documentary commissions. I believe photography is not about taking — it's about receiving. A great image is a gift earned by being truly present in the moment.
